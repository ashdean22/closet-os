You already own
tomorrow's outfit.

Capsule photographs your clothes, tags them, and builds looks out of what is actually hanging in your closet.

  1. 1

    Photograph each piece once

    One shot per garment. No forms to fill in — colour, fabric and how dressy it reads are worked out for you.

  2. 2

    Describe the day, not the clothes

    “65° and rainy, job interview.” The occasion, the weather, the mood.

  3. 3

    Get a full look, and the reasoning

    Top, bottom, shoes — from your own closet, with a line on why each piece works with the others. Keep the ones you like.

Free. No ads.

It will not sell you clothes. The whole point is wearing what you already own in ways you had not thought of.
